# Shopping and price research

1. Use delegate to search several sources at the same time, for example:
   - "Search Amazon for [product], browse the top result, extract price and rating."
   - "Search Best Buy and Walmart for [product], browse the top result, extract prices."
   - "Search for [product] reviews and recommendations."
2. Put the answer in a short comparison: product, seller, price, rating, link.
3. If you know the user's location, also check "[product] near [location]" for local stores.
