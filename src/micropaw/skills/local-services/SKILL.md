# Local services ("near me")

1. Use delegate to search several sources at the same time:
   - "Search [query] near [user location], browse the top result for menu, hours and prices."
   - "Search [query] near [user location] reviews, browse the top result for ratings."
2. List what you found: name, rating, area or distance, price range, key details.
3. Offer to look deeper into any one result.
